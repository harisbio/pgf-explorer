What it is

A structured record of how plant gene families are studied

Genome-wide gene family papers follow a common pattern, but their details are scattered across thousands of articles. PGF-Explorer turns them into comparable records.

What each record holds

The gene family and species studied, the number of genes found, phylogenetic groups, duplication types, expression and validation evidence, conditions tested, and the article's bibliographic details.

Traceable to the source

Each extracted value carries the sentence it was taken from, so any entry can be checked against the article in seconds. Every paper links to its article page.

Who it is for

Researchers planning a new gene family study, reviewers checking what has already been done, and anyone studying how methods and research focus in the field have changed.

How it was built

From a literature search to checked records

The same eligibility rules were applied throughout: original research that identifies a gene family across a sequenced land-plant genome, published in English from 2000 to 2025.

1

Search

PubMed, Europe PMC and OpenAlex were searched with gene family and genome-wide terms plus a list of plant names.

59,838 records found
2

Screening

Titles and abstracts were screened against written criteria, with known eligible papers hidden among them as a check.

97.9% of known papers recovered
3

Extraction

Each paper was read against a codebook that defines every field and its allowed values.

1,848 gene family names standardized
4

Validation

A record was saved only when every value had the allowed format and a supporting quote found in the article text.

Every value traced to a sentence
